We’ve written the first message for you.

Before you confirm

A little clarity goes a long way.

  • Check the title, quantity, edition and condition.
  • Confirm current prices — this starter catalogue uses placeholders.
  • Agree on delivery fees, expected timing and payment details.
  • Ask about cancellation, returns and damaged-order terms.

“Pay on confirmation” is not a promise of cash on delivery. No payment is collected by this website.
